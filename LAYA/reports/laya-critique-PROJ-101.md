## Acceptance Criteria
1. Scenario: Successful password reset. Given I am on the login page, when I click 'Forgot Password' and enter my registered email and submit, then a password reset link is sent to that email within 30 seconds.
2. Scenario: Invalid email. Given I am on the forgot-password page, when I enter an email not registered in the system and submit, then I see an error message 'No account found with this email'.
3. Scenario: Expired token. Given I have a password reset token older than 15 minutes, when I click the reset link, then the token is rejected and I am redirected to request a new reset link with a message 'This reset link has expired. Please request a new one.'
4. Scenario: Successful password set. Given I have a valid reset token, when I enter a new password meeting the policy (min 8 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 digit) and confirm it and submit, then my password is updated and I am redirected to the login page with a success banner 'Password updated successfully'.
